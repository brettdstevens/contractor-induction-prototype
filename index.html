General workplace safety instructions

  • All contractors must comply with Albioma's Golden Rules.
  • All contractor employees must present valid licences, tickets and competency records relevant to their tasks.
  • Copies of licences, tickets and competency records will be verified and recorded before site access is granted.
  • For multi-day work, contractors must conduct daily toolbox talks.
  • Any change in conditions, including weather, simultaneous activities or temporary hazards, must be communicated immediately to workers and Albioma management.
  • Contractors are responsible for identifying hazards linked to their activities and providing documented control measures such as SWMS, JSA and risk assessments.
  • Contractors must clearly outline the roles and responsibilities of their personnel.

Site access and movement instructions

  • No smoking, drugs or alcohol are permitted on site.
  • Sign in and sign out when attending site.
  • Follow all signage and instructions.
  • Do not enter restricted or locked-out areas without proper authorisation.
  • Always clean up your work area before leaving.
  • Mobile phone use is prohibited while walking or operating vehicles.
  • Stay alert for moving plant, forklifts and loaders.
  • When crossing or moving around the facility, contact the loader driver on UHF Channel 35.
  • Seat belts must be worn and all speed limits observed.

Work control instructions

  • Contractors must provide copies of SWMS or JSAs for all high-risk work to be undertaken.
  • Contractors must hold and provide valid certificates of competency for all plant and equipment they intend to operate.
  • Work permits must be obtained before starting high-risk work, including hot work, working at heights and lifting operations.
  • Contractors are responsible for ensuring their workers are trained in and follow all permit conditions.
  • All energy sources must be fully isolated and discharged before work begins.
  • Use clearly identified lockout tags on master isolators.
  • All lifting equipment and accessories must be visually inspected before each use.
  • Only trained personnel may use lifting gear.
  • Only qualified personnel may conduct work at height. The area below overhead work must be clearly marked or barricaded.
  • Working outside aerial baskets is prohibited unless using double lanyard harnesses.
  • A hot work permit must be approved before hot work begins. Flammable materials and waste must be removed or adequately protected, and fire extinguishers or fire blankets must be readily available nearby.

Emergency and reporting instructions

  • Contractors must ensure their teams are trained in emergency response, evacuation and first aid procedures.
  • Contractors are to familiarise their workers with the site muster point, first aid stations and fire extinguisher locations.
  • Contractors must have first aiders and kits available if their scope of work requires it.
  • Be alert to trip hazards, elevated work areas, moving equipment and hot surfaces.
  • Work involving confined spaces or hot work requires permits and the presence of a watch person.
  • Report any unsafe condition, behaviour or incident immediately to your supervisor.
  • Always comply with Albioma / Auminto policies and procedures.
  • Albioma and Auminto management will conduct periodic safety visits with contractors to check compliance with HSE requirements.
